How to read the "Construction sections" column:

  • Each value lists the AS 3959-2018 sections whose construction requirements apply at that BAL rating.
  • Section 3 sets the general requirements common to every rating above BAL-LOW.
  • The remaining sections give the rating-specific requirements — windows, doors, walls, roofs, decks, etc.
  • Each affected lot must be built to the sections shown for its BAL. The sections are defined in Table 5.
Table 5: AS 3959-2018 construction sections
SectionApplies toWhat it covers
Section 3All ratings above BAL-LOWGeneral construction requirements common to BAL-12.5 through BAL-FZ.
Section 4BAL-LOWNo specific construction requirements — insufficient risk.
Section 5BAL-12.5Construction requirements for protection against ember attack.
Section 6BAL-19Construction requirements for ember attack and burning debris with increasing radiant heat.
Section 7BAL-29Construction requirements for increasing ember attack, burning debris and radiant heat.
Section 8BAL-40Construction requirements as above, plus increased likelihood of flame exposure.
Section 9BAL-FZConstruction requirements for direct flame contact from the fire front (Flame Zone).

Refer to the current edition of AS 3959-2018 for the full construction provisions of each section. The reading "3 and 6", for example, means Section 3 (general) together with Section 6 (BAL-19).

Limitations & disclaimer

  • This is an indicative AS 3959-2018 Method 1 screening result; Method 2 (detailed) is out of scope.
  • It is not a substitute for a full site assessment by a qualified bushfire consultant.
  • Automated readings come from public datasets current at the generation date; on-ground conditions may differ.
  • Where automated confidence was low, the worst-case (Forest) class was applied; the signed BAL may be lower after inspection.
  • Reliance should not extend beyond 12 months from the date of signing.
